Quick session

A quick session is a 30 or 60 minute call where we work on one thing. That could be your school list, an essay idea or a Common App question. Slots open when our schedule allows. They come and go, and we cannot promise one will be open.

  • Length. 30 or 60 minutes, online.
  • Best for. One question, one essay idea, or a second set of eyes before a deadline.
  • Tip. Send your question and any draft ahead of time, so we spend the call on answers.
  • How to book. No quick sessions are open right now. Check back soon, or email your question to aktdproductions@gmail.com.

Access Class of 2028 Cohort

The cohort is a small group of students we work with through a full application cycle. The pilot holds 3 to 4 students, so every student gets real time and a plan built around their own goals.

How the year works

  1. Spring phase, juniors. Starts the first week of February. We find your story, sort your activities, build your first school list and practice presenting yourself with honesty and confidence.
  2. Summer. Optional office hours. A good time to draft your personal statement before senior year gets busy.
  3. Fall phase, seniors. Starts the first week of August. We work through the Common App, revise essays, apply to programs like QuestBridge where they fit, prepare for interviews and submit on time.

If you join in the spring, your seat carries into the fall. Open fall seats go to new seniors, who apply the same way.

What you get

  • A one-on-one meeting every two weeks, with more during peak season.
  • A clear task after each meeting, so you always know your next step.
  • Feedback on your essay drafts. You write every word.
  • A monthly group session on storytelling, school lists, the Common App and more.
  • Meetings are on Google Meet. A parent or guardian is welcome on the first call.

How we choose

The application asks for your grades, the classes you take and a few questions about you. It includes a short written response. A short video is optional.

We do not choose first come, first served. Every application that arrives before the deadline gets the same read. We look for four things.

  • Drive
  • Need for guidance
  • Potential in context
  • Readiness

We only ask for a transcript after you are accepted.

Questions

Does it cost anything?
No. Quick sessions, the cohort and the guide are all free.

Who can apply?
Juniors in the Class of 2028 can apply for the spring phase. Seniors in the Class of 2028 can apply for open fall seats. We focus on first-generation and low-income students, and anyone is welcome to apply.

Do I need to prove I'm first-gen or low-income?
No. You tell us on the form, and we take your word for it.

Do I need perfect grades?
No. We look at your grades in the context of your school and your life. Drive matters as much as any number.

When will I hear back?
We email every applicant with a decision within 10 days of the deadline. That is January 25 for the spring phase and July 25 for the fall phase.

What if I don't get a seat?
You can still book a quick session when slots open, use the free guide, and apply to a later cohort if you are eligible.

Will you write or edit my essays?
No. We give feedback, and every word stays yours.

Can my parents be involved?
Yes. A parent or guardian is welcome on the first call.
