Room4Us Class of 2028 Cohort

With 3 to 4 students, each one gets real time and a plan built around their own goals.

How the year works

  1. Spring phase, juniors. Starts the first week of February. We find your story, sort your activities, build your first school list and practice presenting yourself with honesty and confidence.
  2. Summer. Optional office hours. A good time to draft your personal statement before senior year gets busy.
  3. Fall phase, seniors. Starts the first week of August. We work through the Common App, revise essays, apply to programs like QuestBridge where they fit, prepare for interviews and submit on time.

If you join in the spring, your seat carries into the fall. Open fall seats go to new seniors, who apply the same way.

What you get

  • A one-on-one meeting every two weeks, with more during peak season.
  • A clear task after each meeting, so you always know your next step.
  • Feedback on your essay drafts.
  • A monthly group session on storytelling, school lists, the Common App and more.
  • Meetings are on Google Meet. A parent or guardian is welcome on the first call.

Questions

Who can apply?
Juniors in the Class of 2028 can apply for the spring phase. Seniors in the Class of 2028 can apply for open fall seats. Anyone is welcome to apply.

Do I need to prove I'm first-gen or low-income?
No. You tell us on the form, and we take your word for it.

Do I need perfect grades?
No. We look at your grades in the context of your school and your life. Drive matters as much as any number.

What if I don't get a seat?
You can still book a quick session when slots open, use the free guide, and apply to a later cohort if you are eligible.
