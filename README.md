# Room4Us

Room4Us. Everyone deserves a fair chance at 4 years of their undergrad degree.

Free college application and essay help for high school students, especially first-generation and low-income students.

## Site files

- `index.html` Home
- `get-help.html` Quick sessions and the cohort
- `college-101.html` Free guide, College 101. `college-apps-101.html` is the old address and sends visitors there.
- `about.html` About and founder story
- `get-involved.html` Ways to help and contact
- `css/style.css` All styles. Colors are set once at the top.
- `js/main.js` Hamburger menu, modals and home page flip cards
- `images/` All photos, max 800px wide, plus Room4Us logo files. Four squares, one open, for the four years. The mark-icon files sit in the header, the dark logo on the home page, favicons in images/favicon

Text in [brackets] is a placeholder to fill in.

Live site: https://alvinktd.github.io/Room4Us/

GitHub Pages serves this site from the `main` branch, root folder.
