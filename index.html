Free college application help

Everyone deserves a fair chance at 4 years of their undergrad degree.

Room4Us helps high school students apply to the colleges they want. We focus on first-generation and low-income students. We want you to believe you have a real chance, and then we help you take it.

What we help with

This is for the student who has the drive but not the map. Maybe nobody in your family has applied to college before. Maybe your school counselor has hundreds of other students. Maybe you have a list of schools you love and no idea whether you belong on it.

At a glance

Three ways to start

  1. Read College 101: It is free and open to everyone. It covers school lists, your story, essays, interviews, programs like QuestBridge, money basics and what to bring to school.
  2. Book a quick session: When slots are open, get 30 or 60 minutes on one question, one essay idea or one decision.
  3. Apply to the Class of 2028 Cohort: A small group works with us from the spring of junior year through the fall of senior year.

What makes this different

Our founder went through this process recently, as a first-generation, low-income student. What we share comes from what worked and what we wish we had known sooner.

We give feedback. We never write your essays. Your application should sound like you, and admissions officers can tell when it does not.
