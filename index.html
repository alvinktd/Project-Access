Free college application help

From dreaming to doing. From access to opportunity.

Project Access helps high school students apply to the colleges they want. We focus on first-generation and low-income students. Our goal is simple. We want you to believe you have a real chance, and then we help you take it.

Everything we offer is free.

What we help with

This is for the student who has the drive but not the map. Maybe nobody in your family has applied to college before. Maybe your school counselor has hundreds of other students. Maybe you have a list of schools you love and no idea whether you belong on it. If any of that sounds like you, you are in the right place.

Three ways to start

  1. Read College Apps 101: It is free and open to everyone. It covers school lists, your story, essays, interviews and programs like QuestBridge.
  2. Book a quick session: When slots are open, get 30 or 60 minutes on one question, one essay idea or one decision.
  3. Apply to the Class of 2028 Cohort: A small group works with us from the spring of junior year through the fall of senior year.

What makes this different

Our founder went through this process recently, as a first-generation, low-income student. What we share comes from what worked and what we wish we had known sooner.

We give feedback. We never write your essays. Your application should sound like you, and admissions officers can tell when it does not.

Ready to start?

Pick the option that fits where you are now. If you are not sure, start with the free guide.
