Get involved

One conversation can change a student's path.

Our founder learned that from a 15-minute call that ran two and a half hours. If you have time, a network or a school full of students who need help, you can be that conversation for someone.

Volunteer

We are a small, student-run team. Volunteers help us reach more students without giving up the one-on-one attention that makes this work.

The form asks about your experience, why you feel called to help, your age, and your school or work.

Partner with us

Many school counselors have hundreds of students. We can help the students who need more time than a counselor has to give.

If you work at a school or community group, we would like to talk. We can share our free guide with your students or speak at a session for students and families.

Email us about a partnership

Support our work

Students never pay for our help. Support covers the tools and time it takes to keep it that way.

We are not taking donations yet. If you want to support us, email us and we will reach out when we are ready.
